02 · Manufacturing solutions

Suspension kits

Air and mechanical suspension kits and items — including passenger vehicle parts and suspension SKUs for the European and Middle East markets.

Axle components

Axle sheets, torque arms, landing legs and disks — blanked, pressed and machined to drawing on lines from 10 T to 350 T.

Welding assemblies

Cutting and welding lines for jigged MIG and spot-welded assemblies — fixtured for repeatability, checked against the master gauge.

Deep draw components

Deep drawn parts up to 120 mm — covers for axles, hub caps and similar shells, drawn without wrinkle or tear.

Shackles & hangers

Shackles and hanger items, including export SKUs — pressed, welded and finished to print, batch-traceable.

Tool design & development

In-house tool design, machining and parting — proto to production — plus consultancy and industrial job work on our presses and lines.

03 · The facility

12,500 sq ft, run by people who read drawings

Plants J-201, J-184 and J-479 at MIDC Bhosari carry stamping presses from 10 T to 350 T, cutting and welding lines, machining and parting, in-house tool design and a quality lab — run under strict stage-wise inspection. New infrastructure is under construction to add capacity.

Sister concern Swati Industries (est. 2002, MIDC Bhosari) adds parallel capacity in sheet metal components, welding assemblies and cold forging.
